Our Story

SkyVision Drones was founded in 2018 by Mabusha Maake, an inventive engineer from Johannesburg, South Africa. Inspired by the vibrant landscapes of his homeland and a passion for technology, Mabusha dreamed of making aerial exploration accessible to everyone.

Starting from a small workshop in Johannesburg, Mabusha built his first prototype using locally sourced materials and a relentless drive for innovation. His vision quickly attracted a team of like-minded engineers and dreamers, and together they transformed SkyVision from a local startup into a global leader in drone technology.

Today, SkyVision Drones serves customers worldwide, offering advanced drones for photography, surveying, agriculture, and adventure. We are proud of our South African roots and remain committed to innovation, quality, and making the skies accessible to all.
